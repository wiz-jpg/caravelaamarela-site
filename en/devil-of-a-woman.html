Blues Rock · Porto (PT)

Attitude, groove and female-led stage presence.

Devil of a Woman is a Porto-based blues rock band formed in 2023. With intense riffs, emotional rawness and energetic performances, the project stands as a strong option for clubs, blues festivals, alternative lineups and nights where the first impression needs to matter.
